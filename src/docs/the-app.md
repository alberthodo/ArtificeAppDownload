# The app

## Layout

From the top, the sidebar has:

- **Your project:** its name and status, such as **Ready**, **Reading 40%** or **Needs a look**. Click it to switch projects or add one.
- **Search** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>): your project's files, passages and memories.
- **The five places:** Home, Library, Memory, Playbooks and Activity.
- **AI tools** and **Settings.**
- **You:** your name and plan. Click it for your account, what's new, help and feedback.

Documents, briefings and lists open as pages, with **← Back** to where you were. The app follows your computer's light or dark mode.

## The five places

| Place | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Is it working, and did it help lately? It shows your project's status, how often your AI was briefed this week, your connected AI tools, and what happened recently. |
| **Library** | What's in your project? Every file Artifice reads, by folder. Open a document to read it here, or type a question to see what your AI would get. |
| **Memory** | What has been learned? Facts, decisions and open questions, each linked to the file it came from. See [Memory and playbooks](/docs/memory-and-playbooks). |
| **Playbooks** | What work repeats? Step-by-step tasks your AI can run. See [Memory and playbooks](/docs/memory-and-playbooks). |
| **Activity** | What did my AI do, and was it worth it? Every briefing, newest first. Open one to see exactly what your AI was given. |

When something needs you, such as an AI tool needing a repair or a memory that may be out of date, a single line appears at the top of Home with one button.

## Words you'll see

- **Briefing:** what Artifice gives your AI when it asks about your project.
- **Passage:** one piece of a briefing, such as a page of a PDF, a few rows of a sheet, or a section of a document.
- **Memory:** a fact, decision or question worth keeping.
- **Playbook:** step-by-step work your AI can run.

## Keyboard

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> | Search |
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>5</kbd> | Home, Library, Memory, Playbooks, Activity |
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> | Settings |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close a menu or dialog; on a page, go back |

On Windows, use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> in place of <kbd>⌘</kbd>.

## Settings

**General** holds:
- the AI you use most, and how you pay for it, so savings are shown in the right terms;
- notifications;
- privacy (see [Privacy and your data](/docs/privacy-and-data));
- updates;
- uninstalling.

**This project** holds the project's folder, the files Artifice skips, and removing the project.
