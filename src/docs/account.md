# Your account

## Signing in

Artifice signs you in through your own web browser, the same way many desktop apps do:

1. Click **Sign in with your browser**. Your browser opens Artifice's sign-in page.
2. Choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**. You can also type your email and open the sign-in link we send you. If your company uses single sign-on, choose **Use your company's single sign-on**.
3. Your browser hands you back to Artifice.

There's no password to create. New here? The same steps create your account, and Artifice then asks what to call you.

**If Artifice doesn't come back to the front,** the sign-in page shows a code after a moment. In Artifice, choose **Paste a code** and paste it.

From a terminal, run `artifice login`. Its last page shows a code to paste into the terminal.

## Your account page

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Account…**. The badge at the top of Settings opens it too. The page shows:

- **Your name,** which you can change here;
- your email, and how you signed in;
- your plan;
- **This computer:** **Sign out**, and **Sign out everywhere…**, which signs you out on every computer;
- **Delete account…**, which deletes your account. Everything on your computer stays.

## Offline

Once you've signed in, Artifice keeps working offline. You only need the internet to sign in.

If you're signed out from another computer (with **Sign out everywhere**), Artifice asks you to sign in again, and your AI tools can't use it until you do. Being offline doesn't sign you out. If Artifice isn't used on a computer for 30 days, it asks you to sign in again there; using it at least once a month keeps you signed in.

## Signing out

When you sign out, Artifice goes back to the sign-in page, and your AI tools can't use it on that computer until you sign in again. Your projects, memories and playbooks stay where they are.
