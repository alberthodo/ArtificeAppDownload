Who it's for

If you study, it's free.

Three things, and the app checks the first one for you.

You have a school email address.

At a school or university we recognise. Addresses in a department count too: cs.example.edu counts as example.edu.

You use it to learn.

Coursework, a thesis, research for your degree and your own projects. Work you're paid for, or work for a company, needs Pro.

You check again each year.

The student plan lasts a year from your check. When the year is up, check your school email again in the app and it carries on.

How the check works

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. Download Artifice and sign in.With Google, Microsoft or an email link. Your personal account is fine: the school address is checked separately.
  2. Choose Student.On the first screen, pick Student and type your school email. Started the trial already? Go to Account › Plan › Student?
  3. Type the code we send.A 6-digit code arrives at your school address. It works for 15 minutes. Type it in Artifice, and you're done.

What we keep. Once your address is checked we delete it, and keep only your school's domain, such as example.edu, for next year's check.

Questions

Students ask us.

Something else? Write to support@tryartifice.com.

My school isn't recognised.

Artifice tells you when it doesn't know a school's email addresses yet. Email support@tryartifice.com from your school address, and we'll add your school. Then try the check again.

What happens when I graduate?

Your student plan runs for a year from your check. When it ends and you're no longer a student, choose Pro in Account › Plan and carry on: your projects, memories and playbooks stay exactly as they are.

If you don't choose a plan, Artifice stops briefing your AI tools, and everything stays on your computer, readable and ready to export.

Can I use it for an internship or paid work?

The student plan is for study and personal projects, not for commercial use. Work you're paid for, such as a paid internship or freelance work, or work you do for a company, needs Pro.

Coursework, a thesis and research for your degree are all fine on the student plan.

I don't have a school email address.

For now the check needs an email address at your school or university. You can still try everything for 14 days with the free trial.

I've already started the free trial.

Switch whenever you like: go to Account › Plan › Student? and check your school email.

Do I need a card?

No. The student plan never asks for one.

What do you keep about my school?

Only its domain, such as example.edu, so we can check again next year. Your school address is deleted as soon as it's checked. Your files and your AI's questions are never sent to us. Everything Artifice sends, and where.
