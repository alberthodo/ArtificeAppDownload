Memory you can add to.

Write a rule or a note your AI should always know, search what's been learned, and go through new memories one at a time: keep, edit or remove each with one key.

Also new

  • An Artifice account. Sign in in your browser with Google, Microsoft, an email link or your company's single sign-on. Account shows since when this computer is signed in, and lets you sign out everywhere or delete your account.
  • Playbooks that read like a sentence. Fill in the blanks, edit steps where they are, and try a playbook before you run it. Each run shows how far it got.
  • Activity in tokens. Tokens saved, which AI tools asked, the questions your AI keeps asking, and the ones Artifice had nothing for, so you can add a document or write the answer.
  • Connect any AI app. AI tools shows each app with its own logo and when it last asked. For one that isn't listed, follow the steps or let your AI connect itself.
  • Open any document in Artifice. PDFs page by page, Word with its layout, sheets, slides, emails, diagrams and pictures, at the place your AI was given. Search everything with ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows).

  • A short welcome. Add a folder and connect your AI in four steps. The first time your AI uses Artifice, it says so.

Improved

  • File › Check for Updates… checks any time you like. Artifice still checks by itself.
  • Simpler Settings, with fewer choices in plain sections, and exactly what's shared shown before you decide.
  • Safe with any file. Each document is read on its own with a time limit. Password-protected files are listed as such, and files up to 200 MB are read.
  • Better at company questions about policies, budgets, contracts and decks. Overviews of document folders list what each document is.
  • Help with examples, if you choose. A new choice in Settings › Privacy, off unless you turn it on, shares real briefings with secrets and personal details removed. You see an example first and can leave any project out. Usage numbers now also measure how well each briefing worked, and are linked to your account once you sign in.
  • No restart after updates, from the next update on. Restart your AI tools once after installing 0.1.8.

No longer broken

  • Folders full of documents cost more with 0.1.7, because Artifice couldn't see the documents. It reads them now.
  • A link inside a project to a file outside it could be read through it. Artifice now reads only files that are really inside the project.

0.1.7 November 1, 2026 Includes 0.1.6 #

Search by meaning.

Artifice now finds the code and documents a question is about by what it means, not by matching its words, with a small model that runs on your computer. On five real projects, AI tools answered questions for 28% less than without Artifice, in fewer steps, with answers just as good. With 0.1.6 it was 12% less.

claude · ~/acme
Where do we retry failed uploads?

src/storage/put.ts · MAX_ATTEMPTS
docs/storage.md · When a write fails

Finds things by what they mean.

"Where do we retry failed uploads?" finds the upload code even when no word matches, and "tell me about this project" no longer pulls in files that just have "project" in their name.

Tell me about this project.
Claude Code was given

2Map of acmesrc/billing · src/cart · src/storage · docs/

Overviews that make sense.

Ask about your project, or one of its folders, and your AI gets the project's own description and a map of what's in it.

claude · ~/acme
What does our refund policy say about gift cards?
artifice · nothing in acme is close to this question, so nothing was added

Nothing when nothing fits.

When nothing in your project is close to a question, Artifice adds nothing instead of its best guess, so your AI doesn't pay to read something unrelated.

Improved

  • Less noise in big projects. 3D models, game-engine asset files, logs and data files are kept by name only, so code and documents stand out.
  • Big projects are read several times faster. A Unity project took 4 minutes instead of 17. After updating, Artifice reads each project once more in the background, and your AI keeps getting answers meanwhile.
  • The Graph links parts that mean the same thing, instead of parts that happen to share words.

No longer broken

  • Folders with spaces in their names, like Product docs/, could be misread by your AI, which then looked for a file that doesn't exist.

0.1.6 Not released on its own. Everything here reached people in 0.1.7. #

Quiet while your AI is coding.

Artifice now spends your tokens only where it saves them. In our tests, coding agents found the right place to change on their own in a few steps, and even being handed the exact lines didn't make them cheaper. So when you ask your AI to fix, add or change something, Artifice now adds nothing.

claude · ~/acme
Fix the rounding in roundTotal
artifice · a change, so nothing added
Why does it round half up?
artifice briefed Claude Code: 2 passages
src/billing.ts · roundTotal()
docs/billing.md · Rounding

A question gets the code. A change gets nothing.

A small model on your computer tells a question from everything else in about 30 milliseconds, without asking any AI. In our tests it was right 9 times in 10 on realistic conversations, and sent a quarter as many unneeded briefings as matching words did.

Lighter in Claude Code.

Claude Code sees five Artifice tools with two sentences of instructions instead of twelve, and they aren't loaded into every conversation. The "since your last session" note is off unless you turn it back on.

Also new and improved

  • The code for your questions. Ask how something works or where it's handled, and Claude Code gets the relevant functions in full, and for classes, a map of their attributes and methods.
  • Briefings your AI can rely on. Each one says what Artifice did (searched every file, from an index synced just now) and how to use it.
  • Sharper context for code changes when your AI asks for it: source code comes ahead of docs, tests and examples, with where the names in your request appear.

Changed

  • Savings, as measured. Each answered question counts the saving we measured in our benchmark, about 4% of the question's cost in Claude Code. Requests to change code count nothing, because Artifice adds nothing to them. Other tools use the Claude Code figure for now, and the page says so.
  • Composer Pro is paused. Projects that used it switch to Composer and keep their summaries. Settings › Composer Pro › Remove frees about 2.5 GB.

No longer broken

  • "Database is locked" when several AI tools used Artifice after a big change, like switching git branches. Requests no longer wait behind each other.
  • Overview and Savings showed "Loading…" every time. They now show right away.
  • artifice-desktop with an option before the command opened the app instead of running the command.

Built by the Artifice team, after a benchmark showed what didn't help.Read the October letter

0.1.5October 22, 2026#

Antigravity told apart, and a clean uninstall.

Antigravity and Gemini CLI work properly on Windows, and Artifice can now leave without a trace.

Improved

  • Antigravity is found however it's set up (the agent app, the IDE, the command line or a single project), and its requests are filed under Antigravity, not Gemini CLI.
  • Settings › Uninstall disconnects every AI tool first and can delete Artifice's data too. On Windows the uninstaller does it for you. On a Mac you can drag Artifice to the Trash: a small helper disconnects your AI tools, and connects them again if you put it back.
  • artifice doctor shows each tool's setup, which tools used Artifice and how often, and recent sessions, ready to paste into a bug report.

No longer broken

  • On Windows, connecting Gemini CLI could fail with "not valid JSON" when its settings had comments or a byte-order mark. Your comments are now kept.
  • Antigravity showed as Gemini CLI and its requests weren't counted, so no savings appeared.
  • Connecting a tool could leave Windows paths in a form some tools can't start.
  • Savings asked you to connect an AI tool when one was already connected.

Shipped by the Artifice team.

0.1.4October 20, 2026#

Savings that stay, and jobs without code.

Savings add up across all your projects, connected tools show up on their own, and anyone can write a job.

New

  • Savings across all projects, kept even if you remove a project or delete its Artifice folder. Shown all together or one project at a time, by project and by AI tool.
  • Jobs with a simple form: a name, the steps, and the files or notes each step uses, suggested as you type. Templates fill it in.
  • What's new, at the bottom of the window. Google Antigravity can be connected, and Windsurf is found under its new name, Devin Desktop.

Improved

  • Folders are now projects, everywhere. Old commands keep working.
  • Every connected AI tool shows up, including ones you set up yourself, with when each last used Artifice, and which settings file connects it.
  • More accurate savings, and safer connect and disconnect that never rewrites a working setup on update.

No longer broken

  • Removing a project erased its savings history; the Savings page went blank after removing the last one.
  • Tools added with claude mcp add showed as not connected; the same setup added twice gave Claude Code context twice.
  • Two tools asking at once could get an error; "New job" sometimes did nothing; renaming a job left the old one behind.

Shipped by the Artifice team.

0.1.1October 16, 2026#

The first public release.

Downloads for Mac and Windows, updates that install themselves, and savings shown honestly. Carrel is now called Artifice.

New

  • Downloads for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows (x64 and ARM). Once installed, Artifice updates itself.
  • Counted in your AI's tokens. Pick the AI you mainly use, and every count is shown in its tokens. Claude users can measure exactly with their own key.
  • Savings as a range, from cautious to generous, in tokens and money, with every assumption spelled out.
  • Composer Pro, an optional summaries model that runs on your computer, and settings for all projects at once.
  • Anonymous usage numbers, never your files, names or questions. You're told the first time, and can turn them off in Settings.

Improved

  • Carrel is now Artifice.
  • Each project's Artifice folder is kept out of Git, Spotlight and Dropbox.
  • More accurate summaries, a refreshed look, and several fixes for Windows.
0.1.0October 14, 2026Preview#

The first preview.

Where it started: add a folder, and Artifice reads it on your computer and hands your AI tools just the parts they need.

  • Any folder, empty or full of documents, notes and code, read on your computer and kept up to date as files change. Secrets and private files are left out.
  • The right context for each task, within a token budget, instead of whole files.
  • A shared notebook of facts, decisions and open questions, with duplicates merged and contradictions flagged. Jobs for repeatable step-by-step work.
  • Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and any MCP app, from a desktop app on macOS and Windows or from the terminal.
