Questions

Before you decide.

Anything else, write to support@tryartifice.com. A person answers.

How does the free trial work?

Download Artifice and sign in. The app then offers the free trial: 14 days with everything on, no card. Each account has one trial.

Artifice doesn't remind you about it until the last 3 days. Then a short line on Home says how many days are left.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. You only add a card if you choose Pro.

How do I pay?

In Artifice, go to Account › Plan › Choose a plan…. A secure Stripe page opens in your browser. When the payment goes through, Artifice switches to Pro by itself, and your receipt comes by email.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, in Account › Plan › Cancel Pro…. You won't be charged again, and Pro stays on until the end of what you've paid for. Changed your mind before then? Keep Pro and it renews as before.

Can I get a refund?

The 14 free days are there so you can be sure before you pay. If something went wrong with a payment, write to support@tryartifice.com and we'll sort it out with you.

If you live in the EU or the UK, the law gives you a right to cancel within 14 days of buying, and our Terms don't limit it. Email support to use it.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes, in Account › Plan. Switching to yearly charges $59 for a year, minus what's left of this month's payment. Switching to monthly turns what's left of your year into credit, used before your card is charged again.

I'm a student. Do I pay?

No. Check your school email in the app and Artifice is free, with everything in Pro, for study and personal projects. How it works for students.

How many computers can I use it on?

Up to 3 for each person, Mac or Windows. They're listed in Account › Your computers; remove one there to use Artifice on another.

Are taxes included?

Prices are in US dollars. Where VAT or sales tax applies, Stripe works it out at checkout from where you are, and shows it before you pay.

What happens when a plan ends?

Artifice stops briefing your AI tools and stops reading changes in your folders. The app still opens, and your projects, memories and playbooks stay on your computer, where you can read and export them. Choose a plan and everything carries on where it was.

If a payment doesn't go through, Pro keeps working for 7 days while you update your card.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Artifice confirms your plan when you're online, and keeps working for up to 14 days without the internet.

What does paying tell you about me?

Your card details go to Stripe, not to us; the app shows only the card's brand and last four digits. We keep your plan, its dates and the names of your computers. Your files and your AI's questions are never sent to us, on any plan.
