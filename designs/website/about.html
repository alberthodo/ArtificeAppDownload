About

We make your AI start from your own work.

AI tools are quick to answer and slow to find. Artifice does the finding on your computer, so Claude Code, Cursor or the Claude app starts from the right passages of your documents and code.

Why it exists

Every file your AI opens costs you.

To answer a question about your work, an AI tool searches, opens a file, reads it, and searches again. Everything it reads goes into the conversation, and is paid for again on every later turn.

Artifice reads your folder once, keeps up as files change, and hands your AI the passages a question needs, labelled with where they came from. When nothing fits, it adds nothing. When your AI is changing code rather than asking about it, it stays out of the way.

What we believe

Four things we won't trade away.

1

Your files stay on your computer.

Artifice reads, indexes and briefs on your own computer, and keeps working offline once you're signed in. Your account holds your email and name. Your files never leave your computer, and your AI's questions stay there too unless you choose to share examples.

Everything Artifice sends, and where
2

Measure it, and publish it, including where it doesn't help.

We test Artifice against the same AI tool without it, on tasks held back from tuning, and publish what we find. On fixing code it saves nothing, so our home page says 0%, next to the numbers we're proud of.

How we count
3

Plain words, for everyone.

Artifice is for a folder of contracts as much as for a codebase of 4,000 files. So the app has five places with everyday names (Home, Library, Memory, Playbooks, Activity), and if a sentence of ours needs a developer to explain it, we rewrite it.

How it works, in plain words
4

Calm software.

Artifice works in the background and speaks when it has something to say. It adds nothing when nothing fits, stays quiet while your AI is coding, and the trial shows one reminder, in its last 3 days. This site sets no cookies.
