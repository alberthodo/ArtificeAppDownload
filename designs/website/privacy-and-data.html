What the usage numbers contain.

They're on by default, said so the first time the app opens, and easy to turn off. In the app, Settings › Privacy lists every kind of event Artifice can send, and artifice privacy prints the same list in a terminal.

Sent to PostHog, in the EU
  • Features and pages used, when, and how long pages take to appear.
  • How well each briefing worked: how many parts it had, whether your AI then searched again or opened files itself, whether the task went well, and your thumbs up or down.
  • How Artifice is running: how long things take, which AI tools are connected and their versions, and when something fails, by kind and place in Artifice's code.
  • Counts and token figures in ranges, and the mix of file types in a project, such as 60% code.
  • Your setup: the AI model you chose, Artifice's version, your operating system and version, processor, memory and number of cores.
  • A random ID for this installation.
Never sent
  • What's in your files, and Artifice's summaries of them.
  • File and folder names and paths.
  • Your AI's questions, prompts and task text.
  • The text of briefings, memories and playbooks.
  • Error messages, since they can contain file names.
  • API keys.

File names and questions are sent only in examples, which you turn on yourself, and only after secrets and personal details are removed.

Linked to your account once you sign in

When you sign in, these numbers are linked to your account: its ID, email address, how you signed in and your plan, including those sent from that computer before. When you sign out, the computer starts again with a new random ID. When you delete your account, the numbers linked to it are deleted too.

What examples remove

Before an example leaves, Artifice removes keys, tokens and passwords, long random strings, email addresses, phone, card and ID numbers, IP addresses and your own folder name. You can see your latest briefing exactly as it would be sent before turning examples on. The removal is careful but not perfect, so don't turn examples on for projects whose file names or questions you can't share.

Turn it off in one place.

Usage numbers and examples each have a switch. Turning them off stops them at once, and Artifice works the same.

In the app

Open Settings › Privacy and turn off Share usage numbers. Help improve Artifice with examples is off unless you turned it on.

In a terminal

# see what's collected, then turn it off
$ artifice privacy
$ artifice privacy off

With an environment variable

Either of these turns usage numbers off for every Artifice process that sees it: the app, the command line, the MCP server and the hooks.

export DO_NOT_TRACK=1
# or
export ARTIFICE_TELEMETRY=0

For a whole organisation

IT can install a small policy file that turns usage numbers or examples off on every computer and locks the switch, so people can't turn it back on.

# /Library/Application Support/Artifice/policy.json
{ "telemetry": false, "share_examples": false }
# %ProgramData%\Artifice\policy.json
{ "telemetry": false, "share_examples": false }
# /etc/artifice/policy.json
{ "telemetry": false, "share_examples": false }

Or point ARTIFICE_POLICY_FILE at a file of your own.

How it's protected

Encrypted in transitEverything Artifice sends travels over HTTPS.
No passwords in ArtificeSign-in runs in your browser with OAuth and PKCE, so Artifice never sees a password.
Tokens kept locallyIn the system's credential store, or a file only your user account can read.
Works offlineOnce signed in, Artifice keeps working without a connection. A paid plan is confirmed again within 14 days.
Sessions end when unusedA computer that hasn't used Artifice for 30 days signs in again. Sign out everywhere from Account.
Limited access on our sideAccess to our providers is limited and protected with two-factor authentication.

Deleting your account

  1. In Artifice, open Account and choose Delete account… at the end of the page.
  2. Type your email address to confirm. Your account and the usage numbers linked to it are deleted, and a Pro subscription is cancelled.
  3. Your projects, memories and playbooks on this computer stay. To remove them too, uninstall Artifice from Settings and tick the boxes to delete its data.

For examples already sent, or anything you can't do in the app, write to support@tryartifice.com with "Privacy request" as the subject. We reply within a month.
