Why Artifice is a paid app, and why students stay free.

The Artifice teamLetter 2 · about 5 minutes to read

New here? Once a month, the people who make Artifice write about what we shipped and what we learned. One email, no promotions.

Artifice 0.1.9 brings plans: Pro at $5.99 a month or $59 a year, after a 14-day trial with no card, and free for students. Here's why we chose to make a paid app.

We considered the usual shape: a free app with paid cloud extras on top. But almost everything Artifice does happens on your own computer. Since 0.1.7 it finds the right passages with a small model that runs there, and your files never leave. There's no cloud service to sell you, and we didn't want to invent one. What we make is the app, so the app is what we sell, and Pro is the whole app.

Students stay free. Verify a school email, and Artifice is yours for study and personal projects, checked again once a year. A student costs us almost nothing to serve, because Artifice runs on their computer, and we'd rather people learn with it than without it. We'll be honest: we also hope some of them bring it to work one day. People we give an access code to, such as testers, partners, events and classes, don't pay either.

If a plan ends, Artifice stops briefing your AI tools and stops reading new changes. Nothing else is taken away: your projects, Memory and Playbooks stay readable, and export always works.

What shipped

Your documents, read 0.1.8

Until 0.1.8, Artifice skipped every PDF, Word file, spreadsheet and deck as “binary”. Now it reads them, along with OpenDocument, RTF, EPUB, emails and Outlook messages, and your AI gets the right page, slide or sheet for a question. We tested it on 58 questions about a folder of company documents, held back from everything we tuned on:

−13%Cost per question in Claude Code
3.6 vs 5.5Turns to reach an answer

The app changed shape too. It now has five places, in plain words: Home, Library, Memory, Playbooks and Activity. Notebook became Memory and Jobs became Playbooks, and everything people had is still there.

Pro, the trial, students and codes 0.1.9

Account › Plan shows where you stand: your plan, its price and when it renews or ends. One plan works on up to 3 computers. The trial never nags: the only reminder is a banner in its last 3 days.

Smaller things:

  • On a Mac, Artifice reads the text in scans, screenshots and whiteboard photos, with the text recognition built into macOS.
  • Visio, draw.io and Excalidraw diagrams keep what connects to what, not just the labels.
  • Rate any briefing with a thumbs up or down in Activity, and Artifice learns from it.
  • File › Check for Updates… whenever you like.

What broke, or was hard

  • 0.1.7 made folders of documents more expensive. It couldn't see Office files or PDFs, so on a folder of them, Claude Code paid 51% more with Artifice than without it, and got no better answers. Our own benchmark caught it, and 0.1.8 fixes it.Fixed in 0.1.8
  • A link inside a project could reach a file outside it. Artifice could read that file through the link. It now reads only files that are really inside the project.Fixed in 0.1.8
  • Artifice still saves nothing when your AI fixes code. On 50 real bug fixes, three runs each, the cost was the same with Artifice as without it. Versions before 0.1.6 cost 9–11% more, which is why Artifice now stays quiet while your AI is changing code. Our home page says 0% for fixing code, and it will keep saying so until we can show otherwise.Still true

What's next

Teams, later: shared Memory and Playbooks for people who work on the same projects. Before that, we keep working on the code-fix case, and we'll tell you either way. We won't give dates we can't keep.
